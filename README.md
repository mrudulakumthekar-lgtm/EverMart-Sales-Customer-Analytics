# EverMart – E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

## Project Overview

EverMart is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on understanding e-commerce sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and regional sales trends.

I worked with Excel, MySQL, and Power BI to clean and prepare the data, perform SQL-based analysis, and build interactive dashboards that present the results in a clear and meaningful way.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel & Power Query:** Data cleaning, validation, and preparation
- **MySQL & MySQL Workbench:** Database creation, data importing, SQL queries, and business analysis
- **Power BI:** Data modeling, DAX measures, interactive dashboards, and data visualization

## Dataset Overview

The project contains three main datasets:

| Dataset | Records |
|---|---:|
| Customers | 200 |
| Products | 100 |
| Transactions | 1,000 |

The datasets were cleaned and validated before analysis.

## Project Workflow

1. Cleaned customer, product, and transaction data using Excel and Power Query.
2. Created a MySQL database and imported the cleaned datasets.
3. Used SQL queries to analyze revenue, products, customers, and regional performance.
4. Imported the prepared datasets into Power BI and created table relationships.
5. Developed DAX measures for important business KPIs.
6. Built interactive report pages to explore sales and customer performance.

## Power BI Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Dashboard
Provides an overall view of revenue, orders, active customers, average order value, monthly revenue trends, and sales performance by category and region.

### 2. Sales Overview
Shows sales KPIs, monthly revenue and order trends, and interactive year and product category filters.

### 3. Product Performance
Analyzes top-selling products by revenue and quantity, along with product category performance and revenue contribution.

### 4. Customer Analytics
Explores top customers by revenue and order count, customer distribution across regions, and monthly customer signups.

### 5. Regional Analysis
Compares revenue, orders, average order value, and active customers across geographical regions.

The report also includes a front page with navigation to the Executive Dashboard.

## Key Business Insights

- **Books** generated the highest category revenue, approximately 192,147.47.
- **South America** contributed the highest regional revenue, approximately 219,352.56.
- The project highlights differences in revenue contribution across product categories and geographical regions.
- Monthly sales trends help identify periods of stronger and weaker sales activity.
- Customer analysis provides visibility into high-value customers and regional customer distribution.
